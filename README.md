> *"Nothing here is instant. Pigment gathers the way memory does—layer upon layer, never undone. Your cursor leaves currents. Your words leave weather."*

**Project Qweirdo** is an open-source, slow-web generative art experiment and digital humanities installation. Instead of generating instant disposable images, Qweirdo treats the browser viewport as a collective public fresco. Human cursor gestures, five-word poetic offerings, and autonomous randomized cursor drift weave together across a dark plane until the canvas reaches **150,000 strokes**—at which point the finished edition is dated, signed, archived, and reborn as a blank void.
