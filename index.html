About Qweirdo

A small, open project about making art with attention instead of speed. A blank canvas paints itself while you watch, and answers your cursor and five words in colour.

Qweirdo sits between art and digital humanities. It asks what it means to make something together, slowly, with a machine that listens. The logo says the same: one thread loops once, branches like a nerve, and leaves as four strands.

It is not-for-profit and made by Subhradeep Chatterjee. There are no ads, no accounts and no analytics. The words you plant are read in your browser and go nowhere. Your canvas and the archive stay on your own device.

Want a place in it? Add your name under Credits and you will be listed as inspiration for the canvas you shaped.

Privacy policy

What we collect: your name, an optional public link, and the date you agreed. No email, location, account or tracking cookies.

Why: only to credit you on the canvas you inspired. Words you plant are processed in your browser and never sent anywhere.

Where it lives: credits show publicly on this page. Canvases and archive stay in your own browser storage.

Your rights: ask to correct or remove your name anytime at your-email@example.com. Data is kept only while the project exists and is never sold or shared.

Children: please do not submit a name if you are under 13.
