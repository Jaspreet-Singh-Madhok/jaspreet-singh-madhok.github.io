About Qweirdo

Qweirdo is a not-for-profit generative art project. A canvas paints itself slowly, shaped by visitors' cursor movement and short phrases.

The project sits between art and digital humanities: an experiment in slow, collective authorship with software.

How it works. Input is limited to five words. A built-in lexicon maps recognised words to colour and form; other words are hashed to a colour and shape of their own. Offensive terms are filtered and produce only abstract marks. Everything runs client-side.

Privacy. No ads, accounts or analytics. Phrases are processed in the browser and never transmitted. Canvases and the archive are stored locally on your device. Credits entries are covered by the privacy policy.

Privacy policy

What we collect: your name, an optional public link, and the date you agreed. No email, location, account or tracking cookies.

Why: only to credit you on the canvas you inspired. Words you plant are processed in your browser and never sent anywhere.

Where it lives: credits show publicly on this page. Canvases and archive stay in your own browser storage.

Your rights: ask to correct or remove your name anytime at your-email@example.com. Data is kept only while the project exists and is never sold or shared.

Children: please do not submit a name if you are under 13.
